Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Information You Share

When you submit a property review request or contact inquiry, we collect the information you provide, including contact details, property information, comments, and any optional property photos. Please do not submit sensitive financial information, government identification, or information you are not authorized to share.

How We Use Information

We use your information to review acquisition opportunities, respond to inquiries, and communicate with you about your request. Submitting a form does not create an obligation to sell a property or a commitment by StoneBridge to make an offer.

Storage and Access

Inquiries are stored using our website service provider. Seller and contact records are restricted to authorized company administrators; optional uploaded photos are stored privately, without permanent public links. Service providers may process information as needed to operate the website. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Analytics and Advertising

The website uses operational analytics to understand interactions such as form submissions and phone-link clicks. Optional Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta, or TikTok tools may be enabled to measure visits and advertising performance. Where configured, these optional tools load only after you allow them. They may use cookies or similar identifiers. No form contents are included in our custom conversion events.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted by calling 248-800-8100. We may need to verify your identity and retain information when required for legal or legitimate business purposes. Avoid sending information you do not want us to retain.

Contact and Updates

For privacy questions, contact StoneBridge Development Group at 248-800-8100. We may update this policy as the website evolves. The effective date above identifies the current version.
